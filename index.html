About the Studio

Built for
Attention

Camera On Roll Production is a visual production studio helping brands ideate, design, and execute video across the entire content funnel.

We typically collaborate with marketing teams, agencies, and founders as a production partner, delivering everything from campaign films and commercial shoots to high volume social content and AI driven visual storytelling.

Founded by Akshat Bhardwaj, Camera On Roll Production based out of Mumbai, serving PAN India, working with brands across fashion, lifestyle, automotive, culture, and digital media.
